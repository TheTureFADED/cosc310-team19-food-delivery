# Part 2 - Team Agreement

## 1. Team Values

### What values will guide your team?

- **Goal-Oriented:** We wish to apply our programming skills to a real-world application like the project in this course, and to apply fundamental software engineering in a collaborative, professional, and insightful way.
- **Fairness:** We will divide our work unanimously before Milestone 0, and throughout later project phases.
- **Communication:** We will review every PR so that everyone is on the same track and understands the code.
- **Respect:** We will respect each other's ideas and contributions, communicate politely, and maintain a positive and professional team environment.

## 2. Meetings

### How often will you meet?

Once or twice a week, depending on workloads and project progress.

### When and where will you meet?

During lab on Friday and in Discord voice chat.

### What happens if someone cannot attend?

Try to establish contact through online means.

## 3. Communication

### What communication tools will you use?

We will use WeChat group chat for basic communication.

### Which tool will be used for urgent matters?

We will use WeChat for urgent matters, and pin the corresponding notice for the group to notice.

### How will important information be shared with the entire team?

Important information will be shared and pinned in the WeChat group chat.

## 4. Response Expectations

### How quickly should team members respond to messages?

Team members should respond within the same day or around 15 hours.

### What should someone do if they cannot respond?

The team member should notify in advance as early as possible.

### What happens if a member repeatedly fails to respond?

We will leave comments on GitHub and in group chat to let the team member know what is left behind. If we find out the team member is constantly failing to respond or does not complete their task, we will reach out to the TA and instructor.

## 5. Respect & Professional Conduct

### What does respectful behavior mean to your team?

Respectful behavior means listening to others, welcoming different opinions, respecting each person's work equally, and working together towards delivering the project.

### What behaviors are unacceptable?

Any disrespectful behavior, or behavior that conflicts with the group project's main focus, will not be tolerated.

### How will you make sure everyone has an opportunity to participate?

We will give everyone a chance to share their ideas during labs and meetings.

## 6. Accountability

### What will your team do when a member does not meet the expectations agreed upon by the team?

Mutual encouragement and assistance is always prioritized.

## 7. Decision-Making

### How decisions will be made

Proper discussion will be carried out during labs and meetings before making decisions. Final decisions will be made only after collecting opinions and judging the pros and cons of each opinion.

### Whether you will use consensus, voting, or another method

We will decide on it among ourselves based on a team vote, and we strive to work effectively and respectfully throughout this project.

### What happens when team members disagree

We'd converse effectively and exchange ideas over the disagreement to resolve it.

## 8. Conflict Resolution

### Describe the steps you will take to resolve conflicts within the team before involving the instructor or TA

We would first figure out the underlying reason that leads to such conflict. Hopefully, the group would settle and hold a discussion over such matters. If both sides hold a very strong opinion and refuse to back down, members of the team would reach out to the instructor or TA for help.

## 9. Anticipated Challenges

### Missed meetings

If someone misses a meeting, we will share any important updates with them to keep them on track with the rest of the group.

### Unequal contributions

We will divide the work fairly and reasonably among all team members, while taking into consideration each member's course and workload.

### Scheduling conflicts

We will discuss our schedules in advance and figure out a time that works for everyone.
